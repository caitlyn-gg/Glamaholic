# How to use Glamaholic

Thank you for installing Glamaholic! This plugin can act as your home for
glamours, since you can store as many as you would like in it. To get started,
try reading the section called "Adding glamour plates". Keep in mind that you'll
need to be editing your plates at the Glamour Dresser to be able to copy those
plates into the plugin or apply new plates.

---

# Adding glamour plates

- From the main Glamaholic window, click "Plates" and then "New" to create an
empty glamour plate to edit from scratch.

- From the main Glamaholic window, click "Plates", hover over "Import", paste
a shared plate, then press Enter to import a shared plate.

- Click on the Glamaholic menu at the top left of the Glamour Plate Creation
window, then hover over "Create glamour plate" to import plates from the game.

- Open the Examine window a character, click the "Glamaholic" menu, then click
"Create glamour plate" to create a glamour plate from someone else's outfit.

- Right-click a nearby character and click "Save glamour" to create a glamour
plate from what they are currently wearing, without needing to examine them.

---

# Favorites

Keep a list of gear you want to collect in the Favorites tab of the main
Glamaholic window. Favorites are grouped by slot, and items already in your
Glamour Dresser or Armoire have a green check mark.

- Right-click an item almost anywhere in game (inventory, shops, chat links,
the market board) and click "Add to Favorites".

- Open the Examine window on another character, right-click one of their
slots, then click "Add to Favorites" to save the glamour they are showing.

- Type in the search box at the top of the Favorites tab to find any piece of
gear, then click it to add it to your favorites.

Favorited items are marked with a star in shops, on the market board and in
the loot roll window, so you can spot them. Both the star and the right-click
option can be turned off in the Settings menu.

---

# Fitting Room

The Fitting Room lists every piece of glamour in the game, sorted by slot. Open
it from the "Fitting Room" button at the top of the main Glamaholic window, or
with /glamaholic fit.

- Type in the search box to find pieces by name. Every word has to match, in
any order. The number next to each slot shows how many pieces match.

- Click a piece to preview it in the game's Fitting Room window. If Glamourer
is installed, you can preview pieces on your own character instead, and click
"Revert" to undo them.

- Switch between a list and a grid of icons, and pick small, medium or large
icons. Hover a grid icon to see its name.

- Items that only differ by role (for example "Abyssos Helm of Fending" and
"Abyssos Helm of Healing") or by a colour or gem in brackets (for example
"Aetherial Electrum Circlet (Turquoise)") are shown as one entry. Click it to
see all of the variants and preview them one by one. Untick "Group variants"
to list them separately.

- Open "Filters" to narrow the list by dye channels, expansion, job (including
your current job), equip level, rarity, whether you own it, gender or race
locks (including "Wearable by my character"), favorites, or outfit set.

- Above the list, pick a type for the selected slot, such as helmets,
circlets, eyepatches, skirts or thigh-high boots. Head pieces can also be
filtered by whether they have a visor or show your hair, and body pieces by
whether they're a full outfit. Weapons use the game's own categories.

- Click "Recent" above the slot list to see the last 50 pieces you tried on in
each slot, from anywhere in the game, newest first. Use it to mix and match
pieces you've already looked at.
"Hide duplicate looks" shows only one item for each look.

- Right-click a piece to add it to your favorites, copy its name, find items
that share its model, or show the rest of its outfit set.

---

# Finding items that share a model

You can search for items that share the same model as another item by either
middle-clicking (click with mouse wheel) or holding Control and left-clicking on 
any item name or item icon. This will open a window listing items with the same
model where you can link or try them on.

---

# Advanced search

The plate search box can be used to search for more than just plate names. Try
the options below.

- j:job can be used to search for a job by its abbreviation. Example: j:pld

- lvl:maxLevel can be used to search for plates equippable up to a specific
level. Example: lvl:1

- t:tag can be used to search by tag.

- !t:tag can be used to exclude tags.

- id:itemId can be used to search for plates with the given item ID.

- i:itemName can be used to search for plates with the given item name. Example:
i:skallic
